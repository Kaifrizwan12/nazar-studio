# 02 · Intake: 15 questions, 3 rounds

Ask with the AskUserQuestion tool (at most 4 questions per call, 2–4 options each, your recommended option first and labelled "(Recommended)"). Each round is one or two calls plus one plain-text block for facts. Tailor the options to the brand: the option lists below are starting points, not scripts. **Never ask what the repo or the brief already answers.** Say what you inferred instead, and let him correct it.

Before round 1, read everything in the repo and write `research/00_inventory.md`: what you have (logo files? fonts? photos? brief?) and what's missing.

---

## Round 1: essence and facts (before research)
Goal: know enough to research well.

**Q1. What is the brand, really?** Restate the product in one line from the brief and offer readings of its *job*.
 e.g. a coffee roaster → "fuel" / "a ritual" / "a gift from a place (origin)" / "a craft you can taste".

**Q2. Where does the audience meet it first?** Shelf / street and outdoor / feed and screen / in hand (packaging, unboxing) / in person (store, stall, event). This decides which applications lead the book.

**Q3. What's the one tension?** Every strong identity sits on a tension. Offer 3 drawn from the brief:
 heritage × modern · craft × scale · local × global · playful × premium · nature × lab · slow × instant · street × screen.

**Q4. What exists already?** If the brand already trades, audit its existing distinctive assets (colour, shape, character, pack form, sound). Keep the ones customers recognise rather than throwing them away (see the Tropicana lesson in `../nazar-core/references/masters.md`). Logo: keep the exact artwork / refresh it carefully / design it new (a new logo is a separate project, so confirm the scope). Also ask about existing colours, fonts and photography.

**Plain text (ask all at once, never guess):** brand name (and its spelling in each script), tagline (or "none yet"), founders and contacts for the credits page, city and market, products or services with real names, price tier, competitors he wants to stand apart from, languages (English / Urdu / Roman Urdu / others), any hard "never" (colours, symbols, words), deadline.

---

## Round 2: direction (after research, with evidence)
Goal: lock the idea. Every option must cite what it came from ("from 1930s produce labels", "from the founding street's map", "from a historic pigment").

**Q5. Concept route.** Offer 2–3 routes, each with an ASCII preview of the cover and one divider, plus one line of meaning. At least one route must be unorthodox, and at least one must *not* use the default dark-grid look. Restate the winning idea in one sentence and get a yes. **Warn: changing the idea or tagline after this costs a rebuild.**

**Q6. Anatomy.** Propose 2–3 complete anatomies, each combining a narrative device, a format and a module list (`04_book_anatomy.md`), with a 10-line contents preview. At least one must differ from the previous client's book.

**Q7. Palette direction.** Offer 2–3 palettes, each with hex codes, local-language names, a share (for example 60/30/10) and its source (a place, material, archive plate or pigment). See `05_colour.md`.

**Q8. Type direction.** Offer 2–3 pairings with the licence status of each face. If the brand is bilingual, every option must show both scripts. See `06_type.md`.

**Q9. Tone dial.** Where on the scale from sober to playful? Show the same line written in 3 voices (`11_voice.md`).

---

## Round 3: scope (lock the build)
**Q10. Sections and length.** Compact (~24 pages: why, idea, logo, colour, type, voice, 4 applications) / standard (~45) / full (~65, with a social playbook and a full application set).

**Q11. Applications (multi-select).** Offer the industry set from `10_applications.md`.
 Food example: pouch front and back, shelf strip, carton, gift box, stall or kiosk, rider box, social posts, recipe card, sticker sheet.

**Q12. Mascot?** None / a quiet character (a symbol with a personality, no face) / a full mascot with an expression sheet. Recommend based on the audience and category, and see `08_mascots.md` for the test.

**Q13. Unorthodox devices (multi-select).** Offer 4 devices from `09_unorthodox_menu.md` that grow out of the locked idea. Recommend 2–3.

**Q14. Deliverables.** PDF + PNGs (always) / asset pack (logo, icons, social templates) / social playbook / motion stills / Behance write-up / printed proof specs.

**Q15. Review rhythm.** Section by section (recommended) / whole book at once.

---

## After the intake
Write `SPEC.md` from `templates/SPEC.md`: the idea in one sentence, the locked name and tagline, palette, type, journey, section list with page ids, devices, deliverables, font licences and open questions. Show him the section list once, then build.
