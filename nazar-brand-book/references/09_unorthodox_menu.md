# 09 · The unorthodox menu (creative giggles)

Kaif loves a hidden layer that rewards a second look. **There is no fixed house signature**: pick 2–4 devices per book that grow out of *that* brand's idea, and explain each in one sentence of meaning. A device that doesn't mean anything about the brand is a gimmick, so cut it.

Every device must be **quiet** (low opacity, small, in a margin) and **consistent** (it follows a rule across the book), and it should usually **pay off** (a reveal page, a legend or a last-page twist).

## A. Hidden games (run across every page)
| Device | How | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Hidden atom** | The brand's atom is hidden somewhere on every page; a reveal page near the end shows thumbnails with each one circled | A logo detail on every page, revealed on "Where they were" |
| **Page stamp** | A tiny stamp on each page carries a story value (a place, an hour, a batch number) | "BATCH 07 · 09:00 · ORIGIN" |
| **Countdown or ripening** | Something changes slowly page by page (a moon phases, a battery charges, a plant grows) | Dried fruit: an apricot icon that dries page by page |
| **Running ledger** | Page numbers become a tally, a receipt total or a weight | Trade brand: a running total in the margin |
| **The trail** | One line travels from page to page, entering and leaving at the edges, as one continuous route | Delivery brands |

## B. Front matter tricks
- **The dictionary entry**: the name or tagline as a dictionary page with pronunciation, part of speech, gloss and guide words
- **The note**: a short handwritten-feeling letter from the founder, set in the text face (no fake script fonts)
- **The colophon cover**: the cover as a product label or spec (ingredients: …)
- **Contents as an object**: contents as a menu, a route map, a ticket, a nutrition panel

## C. Spec-page tricks
- **Eye-chart type specimen**
- **The logo exploded** like a technical diagram, with labels for each letterform
- **Clear space measured in the brand's own unit** ("one dot", "one seed", "one pixel")
- **Misuse page as a "rejects bin"** or as a police line-up
- **The palette as a paint-shop chart** with mixing ratios, or as pigment jars
- **Minimum size shown at real size** with a coin beside it for scale

## D. Real-world anchors (make the book feel true)
- Real street maps from OSM behind the dividers
- Real coordinates and times
- A real recipe, a real receipt, a real tide table, a real train timetable, rewritten for the brand
- Archive callbacks: one page that sets the brand beside its 1930s ancestor (a crate label, an old ad) with "then / now"

## E. Copy giggles
- Lines with two halves that mirror the brand's tension
- A local pun or proverb, rewritten (Amul-style topical lines)
- Classifieds as the misuse page ("WANTED: logo, stretched, will not be tolerated")
- A fake "terms and conditions" footnote with a real rule hidden inside
- One line in the whole book that breaks the system on purpose, and a note that admits it

## F. Object and print giggles (for printed books and packs)
- A die-cut window that shows the atom through the cover
- Reflective or foil ink only on the hidden device
- A pull-out map, a sticker sheet, a receipt slip tucked inside
- Perforated pages that become stickers or business cards
- Paper stock chosen for meaning (kraft for food, newsprint for media)

## G. Industry starters
| Industry | Devices that fit |
|---|---|
| Food | Field-guide plates, ripening countdown, recipe-as-spec, origin map (source to shelf), nutrition-panel contents |
| Tech / SaaS | A terminal or receipt contents, status-dot hidden atom, changelog as the version page, error-state mascot |
| Fashion | Swing-tag pages, a fabric-swatch palette, a measuring-tape ruler in the margin |
| Studio / agency | Process as a route, client-redacted case files, a ledger of hours |
| Hospitality | Menu contents, room-key page numbers, a check-in stamp per section |
| Services / civic | A transit-manual system, pictogram counts (Isotype), a ticket per chapter |

## Rules
- At most one device visible per page, at ≤15% opacity (except on its own reveal page).
- The device must survive print and screen at the same size.
- Write the devices down in `SPEC.md` with their rules, so a rebuild keeps them consistent.
