# Logo intake (2 rounds)

## Round 1: before research
1. **New, refresh or redraw?** New mark / careful refresh (keep equities) / redraw for craft only (same idea, better drawing).
2. **Mark types on the table?** Wordmark / symbol + wordmark / monogram / emblem / character mark / let research decide (recommended).
3. **Scripts:** one / bilingual lockup (which scripts, which leads) / separate marks per script.
4. **Where must it work hardest?** App icon and avatar / shelf and packaging / signage and outdoor / embroidery and merch / motion.
5. **Refresh only, what must survive?** Colour / shape / letterform / symbol. Ask for evidence of recognition if they have it.
**Plain text:** exact name spelling in each script, tagline (locked?), the brand idea or strategy summary, competitor marks they like or dislike and why, existing files, off-limits colours or symbols (religious, political, a competitor's), Nice classes they trade in, deadline.

## Round 2: after the concept session and sketching
6. **Territory:** 3–5 territories as sketch sheets, each with its meaning in one line. Pick 1–2.
7. **Routes:** 2–3 developed routes, shown at size, in use and with test results. Pick one.
8. **Colour and lockups** for the chosen route: colour options from research; horizontal, stacked and symbol-only.
9. **Lock:** the mark, the tagline lockup (if any), colours and minimum sizes. Note that changes after this cost redrawing every file.
