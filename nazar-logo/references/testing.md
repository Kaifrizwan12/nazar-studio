# Testing the mark

Run every test on all 3 routes before presenting, and show the results as a test sheet (a proven route beats a pretty one).

## 1. Recognition tests
- **Blur test:** heavy Gaussian blur. The silhouette must still be recognisable (a test often attributed to Rand's practice) [SRC].
- **Squint and greyscale:** the hierarchy and contrast still hold.
- **Memory test:** show it for 5 seconds, then ask someone to draw it from memory. Simple marks survive.
- **Silhouette test:** a solid fill only, with no inner detail.
- **Distinctiveness:** place it on the competitor marks wall. Does it stand apart? Could it be mistaken for another mark?

## 2. Size tests
- 16 px, 24 px, 32 px (favicon, UI), 48 px and 64 px (app icon), 180 px (touch icon), plus a social avatar circle crop.
- Print: the minimum size in mm (often 8–15 mm wide for a symbol; test it on paper).
- Large: billboard scale and building signage, where flaws in curves show up.
- Set the **minimum size** from these results and record it.

## 3. Colour and background tests
- One colour (black, white), reversed, on the brand colour, on photos, on busy backgrounds, in greyscale, and in a colour-blind simulation (protan and deutan).
- Spot colour or CMYK reproduction if print matters.

## 4. Material and motion tests
- Embroidery (thin lines and small counters fail), a rubber stamp, laser engraving, a vinyl cut (no tiny islands), a single-colour screen print, signage (lit and unlit), and a fabric label.
- Motion: can it build, reveal or loop simply? (Hand it to `nazar-motion`.)
- In context: app icon grid, social profile, business card, shop sign, vehicle, packaging, website header.

## 5. Trademark first pass (record everything, and always add "not legal advice")
1. Pick the Nice classes for the client's goods and services (WIPO's Goods & Services terms tool helps) [SRC].
2. **WIPO Global Brand Database:** search the name, and search by logo image ("find similar by brand logo") and figurative elements, filtered by class [SRC].
3. National registers of target markets (e.g. the national IP office, USPTO, EUIPO TMview).
4. Common-law check: web, image search, marketplaces, social media, app stores, local listings.
5. Write `research/logo/clearance.md`: what was searched, where, the closest conflicts with screenshots, and a risk note.
6. Recommend that a **trademark attorney** clears the chosen mark before launch or large spend. The database doesn't confirm availability [SRC].

## Test sheet layout
A grid on one page per route: blur · 16/32/64 px · one colour · reversed · on photo · embroidery-style · in context ×3 · on the competitor wall. Pass or fail is noted under each.
