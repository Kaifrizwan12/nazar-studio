# Kaif logo

Read `../nazar-core/SKILL.md` first, plus the locked strategy if `nazar-strategy` was run. A Kaif mark is **simple, distinctive, built from meaning, and drawn with obsessive optical care**. It's never decorated to look "designed".

## Rules
1. **Reframe before drawing** (Bob Gill: "the problem is the problem"). Rewrite the brief until it's interesting.
2. **A logo designates; it needn't depict** (Rand). Don't cram the product in.
3. **Distinctive beats different** (Sharp & Romaniuk). It must point to this brand alone, fast. Generators average toward the same marks (gradient orbs, abstract swooshes, neat geometric monograms), so a mark that looks "AI-clean" is a red flag (`../nazar-core/references/ai_tells.md`).
4. **Concepts with method:** at least 3 generators from `../nazar-core/references/ideation.md`. Go wide (50–100 thumbnails), then 3 routes.
5. **Construct, then correct by eye.** Grids for logic, optics for truth.
6. **Test like it's already in the world:** blur, 16 px, one colour, reversed, embroidered, stamped, at 20 m.
7. **Check before falling in love:** a first-pass trademark search on the finalists, and a lawyer before launch.
8. **Present 2–3 routes,** each with its one-sentence story, shown in use, with a recommendation.
9. **Refresh ≠ redesign.** Keep the recognised equities unless evidence says otherwise.
10. **No AI image generation for marks.** Vector, hand-built, owned.

## Workflow
| Phase | Do | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Intake (`references/intake.md`) | `SPEC.md` |
| 2 | Research: competitor marks wall, category archive, the name, place (`references/process.md` §2) | `research/logo/` |
| 3 | Concept session: reframe, generators, territories (`references/process.md` §3, core `ideation.md`) | idea sheets |
| 4 | Sketching → 6–9 candidates → 3 routes (`references/process.md` §4–5) | route sheets |
| 5 | Construction and optical refinement (`references/craft.md`) | construction drawings |
| 6 | Stress tests (`references/testing.md`) | test sheets |
| 7 | First-pass trademark check (`references/testing.md` §5) | `research/logo/clearance.md` |
| 8 | Present (`references/presenting.md`), choose, refine | the chosen mark |
| 9 | Lockups and the export matrix (`references/files.md`), QA (`references/qa.md`), handover | `assets/logo/` |
