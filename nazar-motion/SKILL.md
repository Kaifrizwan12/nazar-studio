# Kaif motion

Read `../nazar-core/SKILL.md` first. Motion is how the brand behaves. It should feel like the brand's idea in action: the mark builds the way the idea works. Kaif loves cinematic nuance (title cards, credits, grain, the light of a scene), so offer it where the brand fits.

## Rules
1. **The behaviour comes from the idea.** Ask what the brand *does* (opens, syncs, grows, travels, stamps) and animate that. Not random spins and bounces.
2. **Three constants:** one signature easing, a small duration palette, one entrance pattern [SRC].
3. **Real physics, branded:** ease in and out (nothing moves linearly in life); overshoot and squash only if the personality allows it [SRC].
4. **Short:** logo animations of about 1–2 s for most uses, longer only for film idents [SRC, a rule of thumb].
5. **Works muted, works small, works looped.**
6. **Accessible:** respect reduced-motion settings, avoid flashing, and never hide meaning in motion alone.
7. **Storyboard before animating.** Frames are approved first.

## Workflow
| Phase | Do | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Intake (`references/intake.md`) | `SPEC.md` |
| 2 | Behaviour concepts: 3 ways the brand could move, from the idea (core `ideation.md` K: behaviour first) | behaviour sheet |
| 3 | Storyboards: logo animation and one application per concept (`references/craft.md` §3) | storyboards (stills from the engine) |
| 4 | The motion system: easing, durations, transitions, choreography (`references/system.md`) | motion spec |
| 5 | Production: animate, sound, export (`references/craft.md`, `references/delivery.md`) | files |
| 6 | QA (`references/delivery.md` §3), handover | `assets/motion/` |

## Files

- `references/craft.md`: animation principles, logo animation patterns, storyboards, sound, cinematic devices
- `references/delivery.md`: export matrix, developer handoff, QA
