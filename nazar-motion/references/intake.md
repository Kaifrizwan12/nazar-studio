# Motion intake

1. **Where will motion live?** Social video / website and UI / presentations / digital screens (DOOH) / TV and film / events.
2. **Personality in motion:** calm and precise / confident and snappy / playful and bouncy / cinematic and atmospheric.
3. **Sound:** a sonic logo / music beds / sound effects / silent only.
4. **Who maintains it?** In-house editors (they need templates) / developers (they need tokens and Lottie) / an agency.
5. **The dial-up route:** include a cinematic or pop treatment (title cards, credits, grain, film light)? Yes / only for launches / no.
**Plain text:** the logo files (layered vector), the brand book or system, the platforms and their specs, deadlines, music licences they already hold, examples of motion they love or hate.
