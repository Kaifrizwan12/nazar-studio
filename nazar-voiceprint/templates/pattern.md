## 7. Procedure (every time)
1. **Plan** the content first, in plain notes. Don't style yet.
2. **Draft** in the pattern.
3. **Check** the draft against §1, §2 and §6 line by line. Count your sentence lengths, scan for every item in the Never list, and confirm at least one signature move appears where it naturally would.
4. **Revise** only what failed the check. Keep the meaning identical.
5. **Long pieces:** every 2–3 paragraphs (or each scene or section), re-read §0 and §5 and re-anchor on the exemplars, not on what you've already written. Style drifts back toward your defaults the longer you go.
