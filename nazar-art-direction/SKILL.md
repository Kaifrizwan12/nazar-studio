# Kaif art direction

Read `../nazar-core/SKILL.md` and `kaif.md` (cinematic taste: the light of a scene, composition from film). Imagery is often most of what people see of a brand. It must be **real, specific and directed**: a recognisable way of seeing, not a pile of nice pictures.

## Rules
1. **A point of view in one line.** E.g. "Close, warm, mid-action, never posed." Every image is judged against it.
2. **Name what you like in references** (the lighting, grade, crop, composition). Vague words like "clean" or "modern" mean different things to different photographers [SRC].
3. **Real over generated:** real people, places, products. No stock, no AI images in deliverables.
4. **Rights and releases** for every person, location and image, recorded.
5. **Systems, not one-offs:** photo style rules, an illustration style guide and an icon grid, so anyone can extend them.

## Workflow
| Phase | Do | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Intake (`references/intake.md`) | `SPEC.md` |
| 2 | References: film stills, photographers, archive images. Name the qualities (`references/photography.md` §1) | moodboard with notes |
| 3 | Style rules: photography, illustration, icons (`references/photography.md`, `references/illustration.md`, `references/icons.md`) | style sheets |
| 4 | Shoot brief, shot list, call sheet (`references/photography.md` §3–4) | brief pack |
| 5 | On set / commissioning: direct, review selects | selects |
| 6 | Grade and retouch to the rules; QA; library | `assets/imagery/` |

## Files

- `references/photography.md`: point of view, style rules, cinematic lighting, brief, shot list, on set, grading, retouching, releases
- `references/illustration.md`: style guide, commissioning, consistency
- `references/icons.md`: icon grids, keylines, stroke, optical balance, sets
- `references/qa.md`: imagery QA
