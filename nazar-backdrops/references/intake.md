# Intake (one round, all at once, recommended pick first)

Read whatever the client sent before asking. If there's a brand book, only ask what it doesn't answer.

**Q1. What's the source of truth?**
- **A brand book or guidelines PDF (recommended)**: extract from it (`extract.md`).
- A `brand.json` from an earlier Nazar job: reuse it.
- Only a logo and some colours: run a mini-extraction and log the gaps (type, devices, photo rules) as risks.
- Nothing yet: stop. Backdrops come after the identity (`nazar-logo` / `nazar-brand-book`).

**Q2. Where do they post weekly?** (multi-select) Instagram feed · stories · reels · TikTok · LinkedIn posts / documents · X · YouTube · Facebook · Pinterest · WhatsApp status.
Recommended: only the platforms they post on every week. Unused formats rot.

**Q3. Modes**
- **Dark + light (recommended)**: two moods, one layout, rhythm on the grid.
- Dark + light + accent: the accent background for covers and quotes only, and only if it passes contrast.
- One mode: maximum consistency, a flatter grid.

**Q4. Roles** (multi-select) single post · carousel (cover, text, image, end) · quote · stat · announcement · tip / how-to · testimonial · story (plain, text, question) · reel cover · highlights · avatar.
Recommended starter pack: single post, the four carousel roles, story, reel cover, highlights, avatar.

**Q5. Anchor (where the brand sits, every time)**
- **Top-left logo + one edge device (recommended)**: reads first in left-to-right scripts and leaves the middle for content.
- Bottom-left signature: quieter; the logo signs off like a credit.
- Top-right: for right-to-left brands (Urdu, Arabic) or mostly left-aligned content.
- A border all round: the strongest recall (the yellow-bordered magazine), but it costs canvas.

**Q6. Edge device**
- **A thin accent band on one edge (recommended)**: cheap, distinctive, survives every crop.
- The brand's own device or pattern, cropped by the edge.
- A hairline rule or frame.
- None.

**Q7. Small print**
- Handle (plain text, e.g. `@brand`). **Ask for it; never invent one.**
- Carousel counter: `01/05` (recommended) · dots · none.
- Swipe cue on covers: yes (recommended) / no.
- Continuity line that joins up across carousel slides: yes / no.

**Q8. Who edits these, in what?**
- **Canva (recommended for small teams)**: brand templates with locked backdrop layers.
- Figma: components with mode and format variants.
- Photoshop / Affinity: layered files.
- CapCut / Premiere: reel covers and end cards.

**Q9. Texture**
- **Flat (recommended)**: survives compression best.
- Subtle grain (the engine's 4% noise): only if the book uses texture.
- The book's own pattern at low opacity.

Write the answers into `SPEC.md` → "Backdrops", then confirm the skeleton in one sentence before rendering, for example: *"Top-left mark, accent band on the left edge, handle bottom-left, counter bottom-right, dark and light."*
