# The pack

Formats × roles × modes. Each one renders as a **clean** PNG (the backdrop the team uses) plus a **guide** PNG (the same backdrop with safe zones and content zones drawn on it, for whoever designs on top).

## Roles
| Role | For | Static parts | Content zones (guide) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `single` | A routine post: a statement, a photo, a tip | Logo, band, handle | Title (upper 45%), body |
| `cover` | Carousel slide 1 | Logo, band, handle, counter, swipe cue | Big title zone |
| `text` | Carousel inner slide: words | Band, small logo, counter, continuity line | Heading + body |
| `image` | Carousel inner slide: a photo | Band, counter, image window | Caption under the window |
| `end` | The last slide: takeaway and call to action | Larger logo, band, handle, counter | CTA zone |
| `quote` | A quote or testimonial | Logo, band, a big quote mark in the label colour | Quote + name |
| `stat` | One number | Logo, band, handle | A huge number + one line |
| `story` | Stories | Logo below the top UI zone, band, handle above the bottom UI zone | Central content zone |
| `reel_cover` | Reel / TikTok cover | Logo and title inside the 3:4 grid crop | Title within the crop |
| `highlight` | Highlight covers | Background + device in the circle | Icon in the circle |
| `avatar` | Profile picture | Background + the symbol, optically centred | — |

## Starter pack (recommended)
`feed_4x5`: single, cover, text, image, end, quote, stat · `feed_1x1`: single · `story` · `reel_cover` · `highlight` · `avatar`, each in **dark and light**. That's about 26 backdrops plus their guides: enough for a year of routine posting.

## Extended (if they post there)
`feed_3x4` · `linkedin` · `x_post` · `yt_thumb`, plus LinkedIn document pages (cover / text / end at 1080 × 1350, exported together as a PDF).

## Rhythm on the grid
Alternate the modes deliberately, for example dark, light, light, dark… The 3 × 3 grid should read as one brand with a beat. Preview the next 9–12 posts as a grid (`nazar-social/references/system.md` §3).
