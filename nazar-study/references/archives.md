# Archives for visual study

Collected 2026-11-22. Check each link still works and **check the rights of every item** before showing it anywhere. Open access (CC0) items may go in a moodboard with credit; everything else is private study. More archives by topic: `../../nazar-core/references/sources.md`.

## Open-access museum collections (CC0: free to download and reuse, credit anyway)
| Archive | What to study | Rights | Link |
|---|---|---|---|
| The Met Open Access | Still-life painting, early photography (Outerbridge, Moholy-Nagy), objects. Over 492,000 OA works; an API with high-res JPEGs | CC0 for works marked OA [SRC] | https://www.metmuseum.org/about-the-met/policies-and-documents/open-access |
| Getty Open Content | Still-life painting, photography collection, nearly 88,000 images | CC0 [SRC] | https://creativecommons.org/2024/03/13/getty-museum-releases-88k-images-of-artworks-with-cc0/ |
| Art Institute of Chicago | Paintings and photographs; also holds the Irving Penn and Steichen archives | CC0 on public-domain images [SRC] | https://www.artic.edu/open-access/open-access-images |
| Cleveland Museum of Art | About 30,000 public-domain artworks in high resolution | CC0 [SRC] | https://clevelandart.org/open-access |
| Rijksmuseum (Rijksstudio) | Dutch still life (Kalf, Claesz, Heda): reflections, metal, glass | Check each item's terms [KNOWN] | https://www.rijksmuseum.nl/en/collection |
| National Gallery of Art, Washington | Kalf, Chardin and still life; NGA's own scholarly still-life catalogue online | Open access on public-domain images [KNOWN] | https://www.nga.gov/research/publications/online-editions/dutch-paintings-seventeenth-century-still-life-c-1660 |
