# Where real packs live online

Collect the real thing: product photos, store shelves, archive scans. Record the page, the source and the rights note for every image.

## Award and curated galleries (best in the world)
- Pentawards winners gallery (by year and category)
- The Dieline: articles and Dieline Awards winners
- Red Dot (Brands & Communication Design, packaging)
- ADC, D&AD, One Show (packaging categories)
- World Packaging Organisation WorldStar winners
- Packaging of the World (large, uneven: pick carefully)
- Behance curated packaging galleries; studio case studies (the studio's own site is the better source)

## Real shelves and listings (what the customer actually sees)
- The brand's and competitors' own sites and press kits (clean front/back shots)
- Retailers and quick-commerce apps in the market (app tile size matters)
- Marketplaces (many angles, back panels, real photos in reviews)
- Store photos: your own, the user's, or public photos with rights noted

## Archives (the category's past)
- Museum collections: Cooper Hewitt, V&A, the Museum of Brands, MoMA design collection
- Library label collections: fruit-crate and can labels (Calisphere, UC Davis, Boston Public Library, State Library of Florida)
- Internet Archive (trade catalogues, old magazines with ads)
- National and regional archives for the brand's place

## Structures and materials
- Converter and supplier catalogues (pouch types, closures, windows, board grades)
- Packaging machinery and material makers' case studies

## Rights note values (manifest `rights`)
`public-domain` · `press-kit` · `retail-listing` · `editorial` · `own-photo` · `unknown`
All of it is reference only. Nothing from the dataset is placed in client work.
