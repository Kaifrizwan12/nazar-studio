# Angles: every way into a brand

Each angle is one file in `research/` (`01_brand.md`, `02_history.md` ...). Start from the questions; each finding that names something new becomes a new query (up to 3 levels deep). Write the queries you ran at the top of each file so the next session doesn't repeat them.

| # | Angle | Questions | Start with |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | The brand | What does it sell, since when, where, at what price? What does it say about itself? What assets does it already own (name, colour, mark, phrases, characters)? | its own site, social, marketplace listings, press, founder interviews, trademark records |
| 02 | History and people | Who started it and why? What stories repeat? | interviews, local press, LinkedIn, podcasts, company registry |
| 03 | The place | Where does it come from or live? Its maps, streets, materials, signage, lettering, festivals, colour names | OpenStreetMap, local archives, photo archives, travel writing, vernacular-art books |
| 04 | The product | What is it made of, how is it made, what makes it good or bad? | process videos, botanical plates (Biodiversity Heritage Library), trade and agriculture reports, food science papers |
| 05 | The category | How has this category looked for 100 years? What has always been done (the cliché)? What was forgotten? | museum and library archives, old ads, label collections, category histories |
| 06 | Competitors and the shelf | Who sells the same thing here and abroad? How do they look, price, claim? Where is the empty spot? | retailer sites, quick-commerce apps, store photos, marketplace search, the brands' own sites |
| 07 | Category leaders and award winners | Who does this best in the world, and why does it work? | Pentawards, Dieline Awards, Red Dot, ADC, D&AD, Behance curated galleries, studio case studies |
| 08 | Customers | Who buys, where, when, why? What do they say in reviews and comments? | reviews, comments, forums, Reddit, survey reports, census and market data |
| 09 | Culture and language | What do people here say, joke about, celebrate? What words belong to this product? | social trends, songs, film, festival calendars, local idiom |
| 10 | Channels and formats | Where will the work live (shelf, app tile, feed, street)? What are the specs and limits? | platform specs, retailer image guidelines, printer specs |
| 11 | Rules | What must the work carry by law? What claims are allowed? | food authority, labelling law, halal and import rules, trademark search |
| 12 | Papers and reports | What has been studied about this (attention on shelf, colour and taste, the market)? | Google Scholar, SSRN, trade reports, university repositories |

## Depth rule
Each angle: level 1 = the questions above; level 2 = every named thing found in level 1; level 3 = every named thing found in level 2 that bears on the job. Stop at saturation (two rounds add nothing) or level 3.

## What a finding looks like
`- [SRC] Competitor X sells 200 g dried mango at Rs 1,450 on retailer Y, window pouch, matte. https://... (2026-11-22)`
One fact, one tag, one link, the date seen.
