# Dieline QA (on top of core `qa_design.md` and `../nazar-packaging/references/qa.md`)

## Geometry
- [ ] Inside dimensions written as L × W × D, from measured samples + stated clearance
- [ ] Caliper, board grade and flute stated on the drawing, and allowances match them
- [ ] Every cut line closes; no hairline gaps, overlaps or doubled lines (open the DXF in CAD or check the line count)
- [ ] Creases and cuts on separate layers, in the right style; reverse creases marked
- [ ] Dust flaps shorter than half the length; tucks clear the walls; no flap collides when folded (fold the paper sample)
- [ ] Glue flap clear of varnish, laminate and ink in the glue zone
- [ ] No slivers, needle points or tiny islands; inside corners of windows radiused
- [ ] Grain or flute direction marked and confirmed

## Function (on the white sample, with the real product)
- [ ] Fits: the product goes in at line speed (or hand speed) and doesn't rattle
- [ ] Closes and stays closed (tuck friction, locks) in a humid and a dry room
- [ ] Opens the intended way, first time, without instructions (give it to someone who hasn't seen it)
- [ ] Drop test from the channel's height on face, edge and corner (or the ISTA protocol)
- [ ] Stack test (or McKee + safety factor + lab BCT for corrugated)
- [ ] Shelf: fits the shelf's height and depth; stands; faces the right way
- [ ] Reclose and second-life claims actually work

## Artwork on the dieline
- [ ] Artwork placed on the converter's dieline (version noted), not on our concept
- [ ] Bleed on every cut; safe area respected on every panel
- [ ] "Art up" checked on every panel by folding a printed paper proof
- [ ] Nothing important on seals, creases, glue, overlaps or high-shrink bands
- [ ] Barcode flat, away from folds and curves, with quiet zones; scans from the folded proof
- [ ] Braille, TE features and variable-data zones placed per the converter

## Files
- [ ] PDF (layered) + DXF/CF2 from the converter, both the same version
- [ ] Named to convention (`layers.md`); status CONCEPT / CONV / APPROVED
- [ ] Spec filled (`templates/STRUCTURE_SPEC.md`); approved white-sample photos archived

## Engine-specific checks (our concepts)
- [ ] Ran the engine and read its warnings (dust collisions, sector too large)
- [ ] Looked at the PNG at 100% and the contact sheet, not just the console
- [ ] Spec sheet numbers (flat size, area, nesting) copied into the route comparison
- [ ] CONCEPT stamp still on the drawing whenever it leaves the studio

## Draft artwork and transparency (engine design mode)
- [ ] `<name>_art_qa.md` has no P1s; every P2 is fixed or accepted in writing
- [ ] Folded view (`_box.png`) checked: brand reads on the face the shopper sees
- [ ] Clear / metallised film: the white plate (`_white.png`) matches intent; windows have no ink and no white
- [ ] Windows: away from creases (carton ≥ ~10–15 mm) and seals (film); placed where the product sits after transit
- [ ] Light-sensitive products: window or clear film signed off by the food technologist
- [ ] Flow wrap: back art meets correctly at the fin; nothing readable under the fin or in the crimps
