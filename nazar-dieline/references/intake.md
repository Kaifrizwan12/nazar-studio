# Intake (3 rounds at most; options with a recommended pick first)

Facts are asked as plain text and never guessed: product dimensions, weights, quantities, converter names, line speeds, retailer specs, legal requirements.

## Round 1: the product and the job
1. **What goes inside?** Product, how many units, primary pack already fixed or not. Ask for **three physical samples** (or the drawing with tolerances).
   - Recommended: samples measured by us with calipers. Spec-sheet sizes miss bulges, caps and labels.
2. **Product facts (plain text):** L × W × H of the largest sample, weight, fragile parts, orientation it must stay in, grease, moisture or cold-chain needs, shelf life.
3. **What is the pack for?** Shelf retail · e-commerce (ships in own container) · gift / premium · shelf-ready case · display · transit only · several of these.
4. **How is it filled?** By hand · semi-auto · automatic line (ask the machine maker and model: it fixes the style and tolerances).
5. **Quantities:** first run and yearly. This decides digital vs offset vs flexo, a flatbed or rotary die, and whether a custom shape is affordable.

## Round 2: structure choices
6. **Material family:** folding carton · micro-flute (E/F) · corrugated · rigid set-up · flexible (pouch, sachet) · labels on a container · moulded pulp.
   - Recommended: the lightest one that passes the drop and stack tests for the channel.
7. **Style starting point:** a standard code (cheapest, every converter can run it) · a modified standard (one distinctive feature) · a custom structure (die, sampling and line trials cost time and money).
   - Recommended: modified standard. One memorable structural move beats a fully custom box nobody can fill fast.
8. **The opening moment:** tuck · tear strip · drawer · lift-off lid · hinged (magnetic) · sleeve slide · reveal panel · none (open with a knife).
9. **Reuse or second life:** none · storage box · display · refill · something unexpected (a holder, a game, a frame).
10. **Windows or cut-outs?** If yes: film window, open cut-out, or a die-cut shape that carries the brand.
11. **Sustainability position:** mono-material · plastic-free · recycled content · right-sized · compostable (needs evidence). The client supplies proof; we design to it.

## Round 3: production and constraints
12. **Converter / printer:** name, or "recommend". Ask for their style library, board list, die standards and CAD format.
13. **Retailer or platform specs:** shelf depth and height, shelf-ready rules, Amazon / marketplace packaging rules, pallet pattern.
14. **Market rules:** pharma (braille, tamper evidence, child resistance), food contact, toys, cosmetics. See `special.md`.
15. **Budget and timeline:** die cost, sampling rounds, and when the white sample must be approved.

## After intake
Write `SPEC.md` → **Structure**: product sheet, channel, filling, quantities, material, opening, reuse, constraints, converter. Confirm it in one sentence ("A reverse-tuck carton in 0.45 mm board, hand-filled, with a die-cut window shaped like X, opened by the tuck, kept as a pen holder") before drawing.
