# Kaif social

Read `../nazar-core/SKILL.md` and `../nazar-core/references/kaif.md` (especially "What Kaif loves personally": Insta-savvy, pop culture, cinematic). Social is where the brand is seen most often and most casually, so it has to stop the thumb at thumbnail size and still feel like *this* brand.

## Rules
1. **Feed-native, not ads.** Make things people would save, send or screenshot. Design for the 3:4 grid crop, the 9:16 safe zone and the first second of a reel.
2. **System, not one-offs.** 3 templates should cover ~80% of posts. Pillars decide what to post; templates decide how it looks.
3. **Culturally current, never trend-chasing.** Use a trend's *grammar* if it fits the brand, but never its audio or format for its own sake.
4. **Real over generated.** Real people, places, products and process. No AI images, no stock. The slop test runs on every post.
5. **Savvy lines.** Hooks and captions that are specific, witty and local. If a caption could be posted by any brand, rewrite it.
6. **Platform specs change.** Verify sizes and safe zones in each app's own preview before final export.
7. **Long copy changes the format, not the type size.** Body ≥ ~40 px on a 1080 canvas. If it doesn't fit, it becomes a carousel or a document.
8. **Not the book's grid.** The book is a guide. Posts borrow the brand's colours, type and devices, but the design of each one comes from what the post says and what the brief asks. Each is composed for the feed: asymmetric, one idea per frame, no "AI look" (glows, centred everything). Run the AI-look pass in `../nazar-core/references/qa_design.md` §L.
9. **Static backdrops come from `nazar-backdrops`.** The routine-post frames (dark and light, every format) are built there once and reused; this kit decides what goes on them.

## Workflow
| Phase | Do | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Intake (`references/intake.md`) | `SPEC.md` |
| 2 | Audit: the brand's current accounts, 5–10 competitors, 5 accounts the audience loves outside the category (`references/strategy.md` §1) | `research/social/` |
| 3 | Strategy: pillars, formats, rhythm, voice on social (`references/strategy.md`) | social strategy |
| 4 | System: profile, grid logic, templates, motion rules (`references/system.md`) | template files |
| 5 | Content: reels structures, captions, launch plan (`references/content.md`) | the first 9–12 posts |
| 6 | Playbook for the team (`references/playbook.md`), QA | `exports/social-playbook.pdf`, `assets/social/` |

## Files
- `references/intake.md`: questions
- `references/strategy.md`: audit, pillars, formats, rhythm, the "dial-up" route
- `references/system.md`: platform specs and safe zones, profile, grid, templates, type and colour on screens
- `references/content.md`: hooks, reel structures, captions, hashtags, launch plan, calendar
- `references/playbook.md`: the team playbook contents, community rules, metrics, the slop test, QA
