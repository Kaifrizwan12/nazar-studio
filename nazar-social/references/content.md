# Content

## Hooks (the first second / first line)
Patterns that work when they're specific: a bold claim · a question the viewer has · a reveal ("watch this become…") · a before/after · a number ("3 things…") · a local phrase or joke · starting mid-action · a pattern interrupt (an unexpected sound, a cut or a colour).
Write 10 hooks per post idea and keep the sharpest.

## Reel / short video structures
| Structure | Beats |
|---|---|
| **Process reveal** | Hook (the finished thing) → how it's made in 4–6 fast cuts → the payoff → the end card |
| **Two worlds** | Show A → show B → the moment they meet (good for brand tensions) |
| **Title-card film** | A cinematic title card → 3 scenes with scene numbers → end credits with the brand (Kaif's cinematic dial) |
| **POV / skit** | A relatable situation in the audience's language → the twist → the brand as the punchline, never an ad |
| **List** | Hook → items as quick cuts with on-screen text → the takeaway |
| **Topical** | This week's moment → the brand's witty take → done in under 10 seconds |
Rules: front-load the hook; cut every 1–3 seconds unless the stillness is the point; on-screen captions; sound-on design with a muted fallback; a loopable end where possible. Use only licensed music (or the platform's library, within its terms).

## Captions
- First line = the hook (it shows before "more"). Then 1–3 short lines. Then one call to action.
- Write in the brand voice, with local language where natural. Specific details beat adjectives.
- Hashtags: 1 owned tag + 2–4 descriptive ones; no walls of tags.
- Alt text for images; describe what's actually shown.
- Tag people, places and collaborators correctly.

## Launch plan (the first 9 posts)
Design the 9 as a grid (3 × 3) and as a story:
1–3: who we are and why (the idea, the belief, the product or work) · 4–6: proof (process, people, place) · 7–9: invitation (how to buy or join, a community post, the first topical post).
Preview the finished 3 × 3 grid before launch.

## Calendar
A monthly sheet: date · pillar · format · hook · asset status · caption · owner · approved. Keep 20% of slots open for topical posts. Review monthly what earned saves, shares and DMs, and adjust the pillars.
