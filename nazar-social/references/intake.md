# Social intake

## Round 1
1. **Platforms:** Instagram / TikTok / LinkedIn / YouTube Shorts / Facebook / X. Pick the 1–2 that matter most (don't spread thin).
2. **Goal:** awareness / community / sales and DMs / hiring / credibility (B2B).
3. **Who posts?** Founder / an in-house team / an agency / creators. (This decides template complexity.)
4. **Capacity:** posts per week realistically, and whether they can shoot video (phone, a crew, none).
5. **Tone dial:** sober / warm / playful / unhinged (internet-native). Show one post written in each.
**Plain text:** handles (secured on every platform?), current followers and what has worked, products or services to feature, launches and dates, people who can appear on camera, languages, banned topics, approval process.

## Round 2: after the audit
6. **Pillars:** 3–4 candidates with a local-language or witty name and one rule each.
7. **Grid logic:** 2–3 options (a pattern, alternating, colour-blocked, free with a fixed frame), each shown as a 12-post preview.
8. **The dial-up route:** one option that turns up pop culture, local culture, cinematic or meme-native treatment, as the brand fits.
9. **Launch:** a 9-post launch sequence vs a soft start.
