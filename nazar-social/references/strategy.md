# Social strategy

## 1. Audit
- **The brand's current accounts:** best and worst posts (saves, shares, comments, not just likes), and what the grid looks like at a glance.
- **5–10 competitors:** the formats they use, how often they post, the visual codes they share. These are clichés to avoid.
- **5 accounts the audience loves outside the category** (creators, media, local culture pages): borrow their grammar, not their content.
- **Platform culture:** what's native right now (formats, edit styles, caption styles), noted with dates, because it moves fast.

## 2. Pillars (3–4)
Each pillar has a name (witty and ownable), a purpose, a rule, and 3 example posts. A typical spread:
| Pillar type | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **The product / work, in the wild** | Show the real thing in real places |
| **Behind the scenes / process** | Human-made proof, craft, the team |
| **Point of view** | What the brand believes; opinions; culture commentary |
| **Community / the audience** | Their stories, UGC, replies, collaborations |
| **Topical** (optional) | Reacting to the week's culture in the brand's voice (the Amul topical-ad formula) |

## 3. Formats and rhythm
- **Reels / TikToks** for reach; **carousels** for saves (guides, lists, before/after, stories); **single images** for statements; **stories** for daily, casual and interactive content; **LinkedIn documents and text** for B2B depth.
- Rhythm: a realistic cadence the team can sustain (e.g. 3 feed posts + daily stories) beats an ambitious one that dies in a month.
- Batch production: shoot monthly and edit weekly.

## 4. Voice on social
The brand voice, turned up slightly for the feed: shorter sentences, a hook first, local language where the audience speaks it, replies with personality. Define 3 do's and 3 don'ts for comments and DMs.

## 5. The dial-up route (Kaif's taste, where the brand fits)
- **Pop culture:** reference film, music, cricket or football, memes, using the grammar (a poster layout, a meme format, a title card), never protected assets.
- **Local culture:** street and pop culture as a modern language (signage, songs, slang, food rituals), with respect.
- **Cinematic:** title cards, letterbox crops, scene numbers, end credits, film grain, trailer pacing for reels.
- **Pop colour on a sober frame:** one vibrant colour moment inside a strict template.
Say how far the dial is turned (1–5) and keep the template structure calm even when the content is loud.
