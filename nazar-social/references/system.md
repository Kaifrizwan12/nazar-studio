# The social system

## 1. Platform specs (2026; verify in each app before final export) [SRC]
| Placement | Canvas | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram feed portrait | 1080 × 1350 (4:5) | The profile grid shows a **3:4** crop (about 1080 × 1440 displays uncropped). Keep key content inside the 3:4 centre. |
| Instagram feed 3:4 | 1080 × 1440 | Native to the newer grid |
| Instagram square | 1080 × 1080 | Still supported |
| Stories / Reels / TikTok | 1080 × 1920 (9:16) | UI overlays: keep text and faces in a central safe area. Commonly cited: keep ~250 px clear at the top and ~350 px at the bottom, plus side margins (the right side has buttons on Reels and TikTok). Sources vary, so check with the in-app preview. |
| Reel cover | 1080 × 1920, cropped to 3:4 on the grid | Design a custom cover that reads in the 3:4 crop |
| LinkedIn post | 1080 × 1350 or 1200 × 1200; documents as PDF pages | |
| Profile image | 320 px+ (IG), shown as a circle | The symbol, optically centred in the circle |

## 2. Profile
- **Avatar:** the brand's symbol or atom, never the full lockup (too small). High contrast at 40 px.
- **Name and handle:** the same handle everywhere if possible.
- **Bio:** 2 lines. What it is and why it's different (the onlyness statement, rewritten for the feed), plus one call to action or link.
- **Highlights covers:** a set from the icon system, named by pillar or use.
- **Pinned posts (3):** who we are, the best work, how to buy or contact.

## 3. Grid logic (choose one)
- **Free with a fixed frame:** every post has the same border, type or colour band, so the grid holds together whatever the content.
- **Pattern:** a repeating rhythm (photo / type / photo), or a checkerboard.
- **Colour-blocked:** a few posts at a time in one colour, shifting gradually.
- **Puzzle (use sparingly):** multi-post images look great on the grid but break in the feed.
Always preview the next 9–12 posts as a grid before posting.

## 4. Templates (3 cover ~80%)
1. **The line:** a big statement or quote in brand type on a brand colour or texture (with a 3:4-safe layout)
2. **The picture:** a real photo or video with a fixed frame or caption band and a small mark
3. **The carousel:** a cover slide (hook) + 3–8 content slides + a final slide (the takeaway and a call to action), on a consistent grid
Plus a **story kit** (backgrounds, stickers from the icon system, a poll or question style) and a **reel kit** (title card, lower third, end card, caption style).
For each template, define: the grid, type sizes (readable at phone size: body ≥ ~40 px on a 1080 canvas), colour options, logo position, the safe zone, and the editable fields. Build them in a tool the team actually uses (Figma, Canva, CapCut), plus master files.

## 5. Type and colour on screens
- Test all text at real phone size, with a thumb covering part of the screen.
- Contrast ≥ 4.5:1 for text (≥ 3:1 for very large text). Captions on video need a background or stroke.
- Use the brand palette, with one loud colour per post at most.
- Burned-in captions on videos (many watch muted), styled consistently.
