# Proposal

## Structure (6–10 pages, designed like a small brand book)
1. **The situation:** their words, their problem, their goal (shows you listened)
2. **What success looks like:** the outcomes and how they'll be measured
3. **Our approach:** the process in phases, with the thinking behind it
4. **Options (3):** see below
5. **Timeline:** phases with dates, review points and client inputs needed
6. **Who you'll work with,** and relevant past work (2–3 case snippets)
7. **Investment and terms:** price per option, payment schedule, validity
8. **Next steps:** how to accept (signature + deposit)

## Three options (value-based) [SRC]
Blair Enns recommends three options that represent meaningfully different investments and outcomes, and warns against "good / better / best" as three sizes of the same deliverables:
- **Option 1, core:** solves the central problem (e.g. logo + essential identity + quick guide)
- **Option 2, strategic** (often recommended): adds depth that multiplies value (strategy + identity + brand book + key applications)
- **Option 3, comprehensive:** the full transformation (strategy + identity + brand book + packaging, social, web, launch support, a retainer)
Price from the value to the client (what the outcome is worth), not hours. Sanity-check that each option is still profitable at your internal cost estimate.

## Scope clarity
For each option, list the deliverables, the **number of concepts or routes**, the **number of revision rounds**, what's excluded (printing costs, photography, font licences, translations, development), and what the client provides (content, access, feedback within X days).

## Timeline guide (identity project)
Discovery and research 2–3 weeks · strategy 1–2 · concepts 2–3 · refinement 1–2 · system and brand book 3–5 · applications and handover 2–4. Add client review time (typically 3–5 working days per review).

## Payment schedule (common practice)
For example, 50% deposit before starting, 25% at concept approval, 25% before final files. For long projects, monthly milestones. Late-payment terms are stated.
