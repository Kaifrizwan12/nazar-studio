# Presenting and feedback

## Presenting (any deliverable)
- Present live (in person or on a call). Never just email the work.
- Recap the brief and agreed criteria first, and get agreement before showing work.
- Show the thinking, then the work, then the work in use. Every element shown is there for a reason; avoid the "real estate tour" [SRC].
- Make a recommendation. The client hired an expert [SRC].
- Leave silence after revealing; let them look.
- End with clear next steps and the decision needed, with a date.

## Feedback
- Ask problem-based questions: "Does this deliver [the brand idea] to [the audience]?"
- Translate opinions into problems ("I don't like it" → "What is it failing to do?").
- Collect consolidated feedback from the decision-maker, in writing, within the agreed days.
- Reply in writing: what will change, what won't, and why.
- No "Frankenstein" merges of routes. Push the strongest route instead.
- Count the rounds. Feedback beyond the agreed rounds becomes a change request.

## Change requests
Write: what's requested, the impact (time, cost, other deliverables), the price, and the new dates. Get approval before doing the work. Log all changes in `SPEC.md`.

## Difficult moments
- **The client wants to change a locked decision:** state the cost (files and pages affected, time, fee), then do it if they confirm.
- **Committee feedback:** go back to the decision-maker named in the agreement.
- **Scope creep in small pieces:** keep a running list; bundle the items into a change request at the end of each week.
