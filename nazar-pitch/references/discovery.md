# Discovery call (30–60 min)

## Questions
**The business**
- What's happening in the business that made you reach out now?
- What does success look like in 12 months? How will you know?
- What happens if you don't do this project?
**The value** (for pricing)
- What's a new customer worth to you (or a lost one)? What would a better brand change: price, volume, hiring, investment?
- Who else is involved in this decision?
**The project**
- What's in and out of scope? Which deliverables are must-haves?
- Deadlines and why (a launch, an event, funding)
- Budget range (ask directly; offer bands if they hesitate)
- What has worked or failed before? Who did it?
**Working together**
- Who's the decision-maker? Who gives feedback?
- How do you like to communicate, and how fast can you approve?

## Red flags (price them in or walk away)
Spec work requests (free concepts to "see if we like you") · no decision-maker · "we'll know it when we see it" · an unrealistic deadline with no flexibility · a budget far below the scope with no willingness to adjust · disrespect for previous designers · asking for unlimited revisions.

## After the call
Write a one-page summary (the problem, desired outcomes, value, scope, constraints, decision-maker, timeline) and send it to confirm understanding before the proposal.
