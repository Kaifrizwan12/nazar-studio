# Kaif strategy

Read `../nazar-core/SKILL.md` first. Strategy comes before visuals: a beautiful identity on the wrong idea is a failure. The output is short, sharp and usable by a designer the next day, not a 60-page consultant deck.

## Rules
1. **Plain words.** No jargon ("synergy", "best-in-class", "elevate"). If a sentence could describe any brand, cut it.
2. **Evidence or label it.** Claims about the market come from research (`../nazar-core/references/research_path.md`), or they're marked as assumptions.
3. **One idea.** Strategy ends in **one sentence** the whole identity hangs on, plus the tension behind it.
4. **Decisions, not options forever.** Offer 2–3 options at each fork with a recommendation, then lock.
5. **Legal reality for names.** No name is recommended without a first-pass trademark and domain or handle check, and a clear note that a lawyer must clear it.

## Workflow
| Phase | Do | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Intake (`references/intake.md`) | answers in `SPEC.md` |
| 2 | Research: category archive, competitors, audience, place (core research path, passes 1, 2 and 7) | `research/` |
| 3 | Positioning (`references/positioning.md`): the onlyness statement, the tension, beliefs, the competitor map | strategy draft |
| 4 | Naming, only if needed (`references/naming.md`) | longlist → shortlist → checked finalists |
| 5 | Messaging (`references/messaging.md`): tagline, voice seeds, proof points, the elevator line | messaging sheet |
| 6 | Architecture, only if there are several products (`references/architecture.md`) | model + naming rules |
| 7 | The strategy deck (`references/deliverable.md`), QA, lock | `exports/strategy.pdf`, `SPEC.md` locked |

## Files
- `references/intake.md`: questions in 2 rounds
- `references/positioning.md`: onlyness, tension, competitor map, audience portraits, beliefs
- `references/naming.md`: name types, process, screening, linguistic and legal checks
- `references/messaging.md`: taglines, messaging house, voice seeds
- `references/architecture.md`: the four models and the decision tests
- `references/deliverable.md`: the strategy deck structure, plus strategy QA
