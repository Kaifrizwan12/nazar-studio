# The strategy deck (12–20 pages, built with the core engine)

1. Cover: the brand idea as the title
2. What we heard: the brief in the client's own words (3 quotes)
3. What we found: 5 research findings, each with its source
4. The category's codes: what everyone does (a visual strip), and the cliché to avoid
5. The competitor map: two axes, and the empty space
6. Audience portraits (2–3)
7. The tension: X × Y
8. The onlyness statement
9. Beliefs (3) with proof points
10. The brand idea: one sentence, big
11. Name (if any): the finalists, the recommendation, check status
12. Tagline and messaging house
13. Voice seeds
14. Architecture (if any)
15. What this means for design: 3–5 implications for the identity ("two textures", "a two-colour system", "a map-based graphic language")
16. Next steps and the locked decisions

## Strategy QA (on top of the core design QA)
- [ ] Each page states one thing, in plain words
- [ ] Every finding is sourced; every assumption is labelled
- [ ] The brand idea appears unchanged everywhere it's quoted
- [ ] Name and tagline checks are recorded, with the lawyer caveat
- [ ] The "what this means for design" page gives the logo and brand book kits real direction
- [ ] `SPEC.md` is updated with every locked decision
