# Strategy intake (2 rounds)

Use AskUserQuestion: options plus a recommended pick. Facts go in plain text. Skip anything the brief already answers, and state what you inferred instead.

## Round 1: the business
1. **What do they sell, and what job does it do for the buyer?** Offer 3 readings of the "job" (functional, emotional, social).
2. **Who buys, and who decides?** (They may differ: parents buy, kids decide; procurement buys, engineers decide.)
3. **Where is the brand met first?** Shelf / street / feed / search / word of mouth / in person.
4. **What's the ambition in 3 years?** Local favourite / national player / export / acquisition.
5. **What's the one thing they'd never do?** (This reveals values faster than asking for values.)
**Plain text:** name (if any), founders, city and markets, price tier, top 5 competitors, what customers say about them (real quotes if possible), past branding, budget band, deadline.

## Round 2: after research (each option cites evidence)
6. **The tension.** 3 candidate tensions from the research (e.g. heritage × speed, craft × scale, local × global).
7. **The onlyness statement.** 2–3 drafts. Pick one and refine it.
8. **Name** (if needed): a shortlist of 5–8 with type, meaning and a first-pass check status.
9. **Tagline / line:** 3 directions (descriptive, provocative, poetic) with 3 lines each.
10. **Architecture** (if needed): a recommended model with a diagram.
Then restate the locked idea in one sentence and get an explicit yes.
