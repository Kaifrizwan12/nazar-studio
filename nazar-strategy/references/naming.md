# Naming

## Name types (from weakest to strongest legal protection) [SRC]
| Type | What | Example | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|---|
| Generic | The category word | "The Coffee Company" | Can't be protected. Avoid. |
| Descriptive | Says what it does | "Holiday Inn" | Hard to protect until it gains secondary meaning |
| Suggestive | Hints at a quality | "Netflix", "Pinterest" | A strong middle ground, often the best choice |
| Arbitrary | A real word, unrelated to the product | "Apple" for computers | Strong protection, needs storytelling |
| Coined / fanciful | An invented word | "Kodak", "Exxon" | Strongest protection, needs the most explaining |
Other useful families: founder names, place names (check that geographic terms can be registered), compounds, foreign or local-language words (check the meaning in every market), acronyms (usually weak).

## Process
1. **Naming brief:** the onlyness statement, the tension, personality, languages and markets, the categories to register in (Nice classes), length, must-haves and never-haves, and domain/handle needs.
2. **Territories:** 4–6 idea territories (the ingredient, the place, the ritual, a metaphor, the founder, a sound). Generate within each.
3. **Longlist:** 150–300 names across territories and types. Quantity first. Include the local language and transliterations.
4. **Screen to ~30:** say it aloud, spell it after hearing it once (the "radio test"), check meaning in every target language, check length, and check it looks good set in type (draw the 5 best in 2 faces).
5. **First-pass checks on ~10–15** (below).
6. **Present 3–5 finalists**, each set in type on a simple application, with the story, type, risks and check status.
7. **Lawyer clearance** for the chosen one before any design spend. Say this clearly to the client.

## First-pass checks (not legal advice; record each)
- **Trademarks:** WIPO Global Brand Database (Madrid marks; search by name, by logo and by Nice class), plus the national registers of target markets (e.g. IPO Pakistan, USPTO, EUIPO TMview). Note identical or similar marks in the same or related classes [SRC].
- **Common law:** web search, marketplaces, social handles, app stores, local business listings.
- **Domains and handles:** .com / country TLD, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn. Same handle everywhere is ideal.
- **Linguistic:** meaning, slang and pronunciation in every target language and script (including local languages, scripts and dialects). Ask native speakers. Watch for unfortunate words when transliterated or read backwards in RTL.
- **Cultural and religious sensitivities**, and political or regional associations.
- **Script fit:** how it looks in both scripts if it's bilingual.

## Rules
- Never present a name you haven't checked at the first-pass level.
- Never promise availability. "No obvious conflicts found in a first-pass search" is the strongest phrase allowed.
- Keep the full longlist in `research/naming_longlist.md`. Clients often come back to it.
