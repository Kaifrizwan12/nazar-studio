# Every side: one view sheet per structure

Engine: `engine/views.py`. It builds one sheet per structure with FRONT, BACK, SIDE, BASE (from below) and 3/4, from the pack renders and the print art. No AI and no 3D package. Worked example: BAGH (doypack, pillow bag, side-gusset bag, box pouch).

**These sheets show intent.** The converter's CAD dieline and a white (unprinted) sample settle the structure. Say so on the sheet.

## Doypack (stand-up pouch)
- No side panel. The side is thin at the top seal and zip, bulges with the product, and ends in a flat foot.
- The side-seal fin is seen edge-on as a light line down the middle.
- Base: an eye or lens shape (the opened gusset) with its own print, a crease in the middle and the side-seal corners.
- 3/4: the front turning away into the seal edge. Never a fake side wall.
- How the base folds: `../../nazar-dieline/references/flexibles_labels.md` ("Doypack base: in, then out").

## Pillow bag
- Crimped end seals top and bottom. The fin seal is on the BACK. Never draw it on the side.
- Sides are plain folds.
- Base: the crimp seen edge-on, a thin line.
- 3/4: the front turning away.

## Side-gusset bag (crimped at both ends)
- The gusset has its own print with a centre crease: a valley, a lit edge, and the far half a touch darker.
- A V pinch where the gusset folds in, at BOTH crimps.

## Box pouch (flat bottom)
- Top sealed flat with the gussets trapped in the seal. The V pinch is at the top only. The sides are open below it.
- Base: a flat rectangle with a perimeter seal and corner seals. No diagonal fold lines: those belong to paper block-bottom bags.
- The side gusset is the same film as the front. If the front is frosted, the gusset shows product too.

## Honesty
- A face that is not printed or not designed yet is marked "not designed yet". Never invent a panel.
- Unprinted film is drawn schematic (plain tint, shaded), not as art.
