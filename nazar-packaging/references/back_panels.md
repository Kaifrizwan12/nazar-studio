# Back panels: narrow pillow bags (compact back)

Worked example: BAGH chips back, `chips_back(compact=True)`. Mandatory content is in `system.md` section 4. These are the layout rules for when the bag is narrow and the legal text will not fit.

## Grid
- Fin seal: 10 mm, centred on the back. Nothing important printed on it.
- Two columns, each 3 mm clear of the fin.
- Outer edges: 12 mm, like the front.
- Nothing within 3 mm of the crimps.

## Type
- Never below 2.2 mm cap height. Do not shrink type to fit.
- Save space by structure instead:
  - one-line serving
  - tighter nutrition rows
  - legal lines merged into one paragraph
  - short mark pills set side by side

## Cards and the photo
- Cards are bottom-aligned, each with its own height.
- The product photo sits behind the taller card. The card bites the food by the SAME measured amount on every SKU.
- To measure: find the food's lowest row against the paper tint, then place the photo so the bite is 6 mm.
- Do it for all SKUs or none. A bite that varies reads as a mistake.

## Decks
- Deck pages flatten images onto the page colour. Never overlap two placed images.
- Compose any overlap upstream as one RGBA image, then place that.
