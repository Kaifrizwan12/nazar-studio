# Packaging intake (3 rounds)

## Round 1: product and shelf
1. **Where is it sold?** Supermarket / convenience and corner shops / premium retail / online-first (unboxing) / export / gifting.
2. **Range now and in 2 years?** Single SKU / a few variants / a large range with sub-ranges. (Design the system for the 2-year range.)
3. **Format?** Pouch / carton / bottle or jar / tin / sachet / gift box / open to recommendation.
4. **The job of the pack:** shelf standout / premium cue / value cue / gifting / sustainability statement.
**Plain text:** product names and variants, net quantities, the full legal and mandatory text (from the client), certifications they actually hold, barcode numbers (from GS1), target retail price, markets and languages, competitor products, the printer or converter (if chosen), budget per unit, quantities, deadline.

## Round 2: after shelf research
5. **Range architecture:** 2–3 models (colour-coded variants / image-coded / typographic / structure-coded), shown on a shelf line-up.
6. **Concept routes:** 3 routes on the real dieline, each with its one-sentence idea, shown at shelf distance and up close.
7. **Structure and substrate:** options from the converter, with cost, sustainability and look trade-offs.

## Round 3: production
8. **Colour strategy:** CMYK only / CMYK + a brand spot colour / + white ink (for kraft, foil or clear) / special finishes.
9. **Finishes:** none / matte / soft-touch / spot UV / foil / emboss / window. Choose 1–2, for meaning.
10. **Proofing level:** PDF only / contract proof / proof on the substrate / press check (recommended for the first run).
