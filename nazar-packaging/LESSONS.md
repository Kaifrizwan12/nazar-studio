# Packaging lessons

- 2026-11-21: Build "every side" sheets from structure, not from guesses. A doypack has no side panel, a pillow bag's fin is on the back, a box pouch base has no diagonal folds. Mark undesigned faces "not designed yet". Rules: `references/views.md`.
- 2026-11-21: A doypack base gusset folds in, then out. Draw it as a W when empty, a floor when filled. Rules: `../nazar-dieline/references/flexibles_labels.md`.
- 2026-11-21: On narrow pillow bags, keep type at 2.2 mm cap or more and save space by structure. Make the photo bite the same on every SKU. Never overlap two placed images in a deck. Rules: `references/back_panels.md`.
- 2026-11-21: In a case-study deck, never reuse a layout device or show the same pack image twice. Give each page its own composition, scale or colour, and show every pack's front and back. Check the full contact sheet for repeats before you show it. Compose overlaps (couples, fans) into one RGBA so the deck's overlap guard still passes.
- 2026-11-21: Keep pack art clean in decks. No numbered callouts on the pack, and no AI scene photos unless the owner picks one; here only the mango flat-lay was kept.
- 2026-11-23 · Pack mockups read as plastic / CG · flat art with a painted sheen, then a perfect cube: no hinge fold, a moulded radius, flat panels, a mirror coat, pin-sharp focus. Each cost a review round. · references/render_3d.md: dieline geometry as board shells, a board-realism checklist, materials, lens DOF, product-table light; plus "subtract luxury codes".
