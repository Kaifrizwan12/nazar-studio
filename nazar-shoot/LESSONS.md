# Shoot lessons

- 2026-11-21: Hybrid rule. AI made only the empty plate; the packs were our renders, so type stayed sharp. Every AI output carried the disclosure stamp. Engine: `engine/composite.py`.
- 2026-11-21: The owner called floating packs, pale ghost shadows and creaseless film "AI slop". Fix the physics, not the strength: a tight contact shadow at the base, a projected cast shadow that softens with distance, the plate's black level and colour cast on the pack.
- 2026-11-21: Packs standing behind a shelf lip need the plate's lip band pasted back over their bases (`--relay`). Without it they read as stickers.
- 2026-11-21: Hyper-real scenes are parked unless the owner asks. AI scene photos were dropped from the deck; only a flat-lay the owner picked stayed.
- 2026-11-21: No "chic slop" plates (palm shadows, travertine, beige plinths). Brief real local places with one named light.
- 2026-11-21: Building the self-test showed that a flat-topped projected shadow reads like a cut-out slab. The penumbra must widen and fade toward the tip, and the near edge needs room to blur below the base.
- 2026-11-21: Never mirror a pack render to fake a second unit. The type reads backwards. Use another SKU's render.
- 2026-11-21: A soft drop shadow alone melted the pouch's seal teeth and notches into a blur. Add a tight contact shadow under the soft one, in films and in every composite.
- 2026-11-21: Cinematic films: a wide feathered wipe ghosted two packs into each other (doubled type). A crisp edge with a light glow reads as a light leak and never blends packs. Show stills of the start, mid-shot, mid-wipe and the next shot before rendering video.
- 2026-11-21: The flavour-cycle film was a one-brand script. It is now `engine/flavour_cycle.py`, driven by a JSON job (any range, any aspect, platform presets with safe zones, cinematic or snap, loop, beat sync, optional music). Tint `auto` samples the pack's dominant print colour.
- 2026-11-21: Vertical presets (Reels, Stories, TikTok, Shorts) shrink the pack to fit the safe zone instead of letting the UI cover the pack's type. Check the stills sheet: the safe zone is drawn on the mid-shot.
- 2026-11-21: Flash safety is measured on every render (WCAG 2.3.1 general and red flashes over 11.1% of the frame). Shots are never shorter than fps/3 frames, and the wipe glow is capped at +48 levels on a thin band, so a range that swings dark to light tints stays at 1.5 flashes a second or less. The self-test shows the meter fails a real strobe.
- 2026-11-21: For a loop, the last frame is a pixel-exact copy of the first (no hero repeat); the tests compare them byte for byte.
- 2026-11-22: The keystone sign was backwards for a camera above an upright pack: the top is nearer the lens, so it reads slightly WIDER. `composite.py --persp` now takes a signed value (negative narrows the base); `shotplan.py` computes it from camera height, tilt and how far the pack leans back. The default (0.03) is unchanged, so old jobs render the same.
- 2026-11-22: Plan before prompt. `shotplan.py` turns a camera, light and finish spec into numbers, a diagram, warnings (glare on the print, two shadows, mired clash, wide lens, depth of field) and the plate prompt. A satin face's glare showed up at the planned key position on the example spec; the planner moved the key up 20 degrees to clear it.
- 2026-11-22: Proxy route for Gemini and other models: brief a blank, unprinted stand-in of the pack's shape; `proxy.py` keeps the model's shadows and reflections and puts our exact pixels in with the stand-in's shading.
- 2026-11-22: `shotplan.py` crashed on a sun light (distance 1.5e11 m): inverse-square illuminance rounded to 0 and log2 failed; the diagram drew the sun off the page. Now `kind: "sun"` (or any light past 10 km) is parallel light: no falloff across the set, `power` = its relative illuminance, and diagrams clamp far lights to the edge.
- 2026-11-22: The plate prompt always said "verticals straight", even for a camera tilted 42 degrees up. Above 12 degrees of tilt it now asks for naturally converging verticals.
- 2026-11-22: Hand-held shots (packs raised to the sky, a basket in a hand, a tear-open macro) need the proxy route with occlusion: `proxy.py --occlude` keeps anything coloured in front of a neutral stand-in (skin, wicker, plastic mesh) and reads the light only from the visible stand-in; `--keep mask.png` covers neutral occluders; `--quad` warps the print onto a tilted or rotated stand-in. Ask the model for an unprinted matte white stand-in so colour separates hand from pack. Self-tests added.
- 2026-11-22: Match composited cast shadows to the plate's OWN shadows from objects at the same distance from the surface (measure their 10-90% edge width and shadow/lit ratio), not to a guessed blur. On a sunlit wall the plate's peg shadows gave sigma about 2.3 px and a neutral 0.656 ratio; the old blur of 7 px and 0.55 read as grey cards. A pack full of fruit transmits almost nothing through its window, so the coloured glow in its shadow stays faint.
- 2026-11-22: First real hand-held proxy test (three blank pouches held against a blue sky). Eyeballed quad corners left a white stand-in fringe, and grey patches showed beside the fingers. Two fixes in `proxy.py`: `--clip` cuts the oversized print to the stand-in's exact drawn outline (hole-filled, so wrinkle shadows stay in); and both the outline and the occluder test treat cool grey (b >= r, chroma < 0.40) as the stand-in in shade, because a white pack's finger shadows take the sky's blue (measured at chroma 0.24). Skin leans red; open sky is chroma > 0.8. Use `--occlude --clip --quad` for every hand-held shot.
- 2026-11-22: Plate-prompt craft: three packs at one height, one tilt and one spacing read as a posed line-up. Ask for different depths, overlap and a gesture (a toast). Small props named in the prompt (a wrist thread, cables) get drawn literally and can look cheap, so name only what the shot needs.
- 2026-11-22: Owner's note on a hands-to-the-sky plate: "why would they pose up in the sky?" A borrowed gesture (a drinks brand's toast) has no reason to exist for a snack. Every hand in a shot needs a motive the product gives it: opening, sharing, picking from a basket, cooking, snacking during an activity people really do. Test before prompting: "what is this hand doing, and would it do that without a camera?"
- 2026-11-22: On a warm plate (white stand-in torn open in two hands against an orange wall) colour rules failed: the stand-in in warm shade (119,99,84) is the same colour as skin in shade (89,72,63), so the cut round the fingers left grey holes. No colour threshold separates them. Fix: hand-held shots use a chroma-green stand-in (`proxy.py --key green`): the stand-in is anything strongly green, the occluder is everything else that touches the outside, shading is read from the max channel with no tint, and green spill on nearby skin is clamped. An existing white-stand-in plate can be turned green with an image-model edit ("change only the pouch to flat chroma green, keep every fold and shadow, keep everything else the same").
- 2026-11-22: Green-key compositing broke on a GREEN pack (a dark-green chips bag): the clean-up pass hunted green in the finished image and wiped our own art, and each later pack reread earlier packs' green art as stand-in. Rule: read every key, silhouette and occluder test from the untouched plate; paint onto a separate canvas (`apply(..., canvas=im)`); never overwrite pixels an earlier pack painted (composite the front pack first with `quad_grow=0`); `recolor_key(..., plate=, region=)` touches only pixels still equal to the plate's key, near the packs (otherwise green products on blurred shelves get repainted).
- 2026-11-22: Teal is still key: green in deep shade under a blue sky drifts to G ~ B, and hand shadow on green goes nearly black (G ~ 15). The key test is G > 1.25 R and G > 0.92 B and G > 10; the shading floor drops to 0.18 in key mode so deep shade stays deep.
- 2026-11-22: Route A on a backlit plate: `composite.py` reads its gain from the plate behind the pack (dark backlit foliage) and turned an orange pouch muddy brown. For backlight, lower `light_k` (~0.45), keep `sat` at 1.0, add a warm rim on the edges that face the sun, and match the pack's softness to the plate's (an upscaled plate needs `soft` ~0.85). Always compare the pack's colour with a real object of similar colour in the plate (here, real apricots).
- 2026-11-22: Downloads named `.jpeg` from the image model came at preview size (928x1152) while `.jpg` downloads were full size (1856x2304). Ask for the full-size download.
- 2026-11-22: Owner's verdict on a five-shot set: "only the hand and the hanging are ok, the others very very bad". Cause: my QA compared each attempt with the previous one, so every step looked like progress, never with a real photo. Now six realism gates in SKILL.md (beside a real photo at 100%; one or two packs; the whole moment; motivated gesture; flat art only where the pack is flat; zoom every edge and overlap).
- 2026-11-22: Multi-pack AI scenes (three packs in a tight basket crop, a crate, a stove) never passed. The plate that passed showed the whole moment: a person's arm, clothes and the aisle, two packs in a basket. Brief the moment wide, with one or two packs.
- 2026-11-22: Bananas in front of a green stand-in went see-through: yellow-green fruit passed the key test. #00B140 carries blue (B/G ~0.55) and almost no red; banana and leaf carry real red and no blue. Key now also needs B > 0.3 G or G > 2.5 R (deep creases). Self-tested.
- 2026-11-22: `soften` and `displace` blurred the art's RGB with black from its transparent pixels: a dark navy rim on a bag edge. The art colour is now edge-extended into the transparent area first. Self-tested.
- 2026-11-22: A stand-in taller than our pack shows through a basket's mesh below the rim: fill those pixels with the pack's body colour in the basket's shade (calmer saturation, plate luminance, 1.5 px feather), and only in the band under the rim, or the light floor tiles get painted too. Always restore the plate's red rim (dilated) last, since its white glints pass as stand-in.
- 2026-11-22: A haze I blamed on new grading flags was in the plate's own sheen: measure before and after (region mean, p2, std) before calling a change better or worse; small side-by-side thumbnails lie.
- 2026-11-22: The cinematic film's white light sweep over the pack read as a cheap "glaze" (owner review). The sweep is now off by default (SWEEP 0); the look comes from lighting the room instead: a key-light pool behind the pack that never brightens past the tint (a +16% pool clipped yellow), deeper vignette, a gentle S-curve, a shallower depth of field on the type wall, a crisper pack and a thinner wipe edge (glow 26). Compare before/after stills of two tints before rendering.
