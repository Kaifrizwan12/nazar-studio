# Story-driven scenes

Hyper-real scenes are **parked unless the owner asks**. When asked, each scene tells one small true story about where the product lives; the pack is the reason the frame exists, not a prop in a mood.

## Start from the story
Write one line before any plate: **who**, **where**, **when**, **what just happened**, and **why the pack is there**.
- "A student's exam desk at 1 am, the pouch torn open next to past papers."
- "Packs blocked on a kiryana shelf at dusk, tube light, a hand reaching for the second row."
- "Fruit sun-drying on a rooftop charpai; the pouch lying where someone set it down."
If the line could describe any brand's product, it is not a story yet.

## Local life, with restraint
Use real places and habits: kiryana shelves, supermarket bays, rooftops, rickshaw seats, school bags, chai breaks, dupatta corners, steel tiffins. Use them as setting, never as costume. No faces in AI plates; hands are fine.

## Hands
- A hand says use: reaching, tearing the notch, tipping fruit into a palm. One hand, cropped at the wrist or forearm.
- Hands come from the plate (briefed with an empty grip space) or a real photo; never generated touching our pack, because the model will redraw it.
- Fingers in front of the pack are a re-lay: mask them from the plate and paste them back over the product.

## Sky and light
- Name the hour and the sky: "noon, hard sun, clear sky" makes short dark shadows; "dusk, overcast" makes long soft ones and a cool cast. The product's shadow and sheen follow the same light (`plates.md`).
- Outdoors, the sky colour reaches the pack's top edges: light wrap does this; check it is cool on top, warm from the ground.

## Composition
- Off-centre, cropped, layered: something in front, the product, something behind. Never a lone dead-centre product on an empty sweep.
- Multi-packs from real renders only (blocked facings, units stocked behind, slight lean variation); never hand-drawn pack shapes.
- Text only where the story needs it (a shelf strip, a header board), drawn by code in the brand's real type and lit by the plate.

## Never
- Other brands' packs, logos or shop names in frame.
- Floating or exploding product, splash shots, glowing halos, levitating fruit.
- Generated fruit pretending to be the product inside a window.
- AI scene photos in the deck unless the owner picks one.
