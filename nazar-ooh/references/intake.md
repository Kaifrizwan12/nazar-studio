# OOH intake

## Round 1
1. **Goal:** launch awareness / a local footfall drive / an event / a brand statement / a sales promotion.
2. **Media:** billboards / transit and vehicles / walls and murals / street posters / digital screens / in-venue / guerrilla / a mix (send the media plan).
3. **Audience movement:** drivers at speed / passengers / pedestrians / a captive audience (waiting, in-venue).
4. **Screen twin:** social / geo-targeted ads / QR experience / press stunt / none.
5. **Risk appetite (for guerrilla):** safe and permitted only / bold but legal / let's discuss.
**Plain text:** the site list with photos, sizes, locations and orientation (from the media owner), the media owner's specs and deadlines, campaign dates, mandatory items (logo, legal lines, URL), budget for production, the languages of the area.

## Round 2: after concepts
6. **Route choice:** 3 routes, each shown on a real site photo at real proportion, with its screen twin.
7. **Adaptation set:** which formats get which version (the full idea vs a simplified one).
8. **The dial:** how far to push the pop culture, local wit and cinematic treatment.
