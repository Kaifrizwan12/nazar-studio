# Production, installation and records

## 1. Files
- Get the **media owner's spec sheet**: final size, the visible area vs bleed, resolution (large formats are often supplied at a fraction of full size, e.g. 1:10, at a stated dpi), colour mode and profile, file format, and delivery deadline.
- Vector for type and logos; images at the required effective resolution (large-format viewing distance allows lower ppi at full size, but follow the spec).
- Mark the safe area: frames, mullions, seams and panel joins (billboards are often printed in panels), and vehicle features (windows, handles, lights, hinges).
- DOOH: exact pixel dimensions, frame rate, duration, file type and size limits, and brightness and content rules. Test on the real screen if possible.

## 2. Materials
Paper posters · vinyl (flex) banners · mesh (for windy, large sites) · adhesive vinyl for walls, vehicles and windows (cast vs calendared for curves) · reflective films for night · hand-painting for murals and walls. Ask the printer which material suits each site's sun, rain and lifespan.

## 3. Installation and permits
- Permits from the local authority or the property owner where needed (walls, murals, events, guerrilla).
- An installation spec per site: material, size, fixing method, orientation photo, access notes.
- Safety: no obstruction of roads, signals or sightlines; certified installers for heights.
- A removal and clean-up plan, especially for temporary and guerrilla work.

## 4. Documentation (for the case study)
Photograph every site after installation (day and night, wide and close, with people for scale), collect social posts and press, record what you can measure (scans, mentions, search spikes, footfall), and write a short case study (problem → idea → execution → outcome).

## 5. OOH QA (on top of core `qa_design.md`)
- [ ] One idea per board; the word count fits the speed of the site
- [ ] Letter heights checked against viewing distance (the generous rule) and tested at thumbnail size
- [ ] Contrast checked in greyscale, day and night
- [ ] Every format re-composed, not just scaled; the master layout sheet checked side by side
- [ ] Safe areas respect seams, frames and vehicle features
- [ ] The screen twin is designed and the QR or link tested at the real distance
- [ ] Legal lines, permits and media-owner specs confirmed
- [ ] Guerrilla ideas are legal, safe and approved in writing
- [ ] Files delivered to spec (size, scale, resolution, profile, naming)
- [ ] Installation photographed and the case study drafted
