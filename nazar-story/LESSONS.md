# Story lessons

- 2026-11-21: Show every product's front and back exactly once. Never reuse a layout device or the same pack image in one story. Compose overlaps (front-back pairs, fans, tosses) as one RGBA, so the overlap guard still passes.
- 2026-11-21: Keep pack art clean on story pages. No numbered callouts or arrows on the pack. Explain the rules beside the pack, as a list.
- 2026-11-21: Use no AI scene photos unless the owner picks one. Here one flat-lay was kept and the rest were parked.
- 2026-11-21: Include a measured flats page (every structure with grams and mm). It reads as a real job and answers the printer's first question.
- 2026-11-21: The studio contact page (mark, one line, founder, email, phone, social) goes before thank-you, never after it. Use owner-supplied details only.
- 2026-11-21: Show the full contact sheet before shipping. The owner reviews by page number, so fix that page, then hunt the same class of problem on every page.
- 2026-11-21: Product groups were hand-built per job (fan, couple, till row, bleed hero, padded flat-lay), each with its own shadow offset, so shadows on one page fell different ways. Now `engine/composers.py` is generic: one shared `Light`, soft plus tight contact shadow from the silhouette, true scale by mm, a device log. Self-test and `test_composers.py` pass.
- 2026-11-21: A flat colour fill beside a lit gradient showed a Mach band at the seam on the first contact sheet. `extend_canvas` now carries the edge's falloff on and eases it flat, with matched grain.
- 2026-11-21: An overlap row with the right item on top hid every tuck shadow (the light was from the left). `row(on_top="auto")` puts the item nearer the light on top, so each tuck casts onto its neighbour.
- 2026-11-21: A stack placed by bounding boxes floated with gaps between tilted items. `stack` now drops each item until its silhouette touches the pile.
- 2026-11-22: The Behance cut had no written project text (description, fields, tags, tools, credits, status). Curators weigh context and credits; the playbook now writes it with the panels. Panels also get a measured consistency pass with nazar-study's look.py.
- 2026-11-22: Owner on a packaging deck: box views sat in visible rectangles (each render had its own grey-beige sweep), a family page was a pasted QA contact sheet on grey with tiny captions, and the structure pages "just put in". Fixed by re-setting sweeps onto the page colour, laying flats as multiply, composing the family as cutouts, and showing each structure beside its flat. Rules 15-17.
- 2026-11-22: New shoot photos were first added as one page near the end; owner: "used it as trash at last". A picked photo goes where it carries the story (after the idea page). Its text panel "did not sit": replaced by a wide + 1:1 detail diptych. Rule 18.
- 2026-11-22: Mango led seven pages (cover, idea photo, tear-open, dieline, family code, hero, chips hero). Hero moments moved to kiwi and strawberry. Rule 19.
- 2026-11-22: A stacked tagline lockup put a comma at a line end; its tail hit the cap of the line below ("PHAL" read "PHAL'"). Stacked lockups now add 0.32 x cap under a line that ends in a comma.
- 2026-11-22: Re-setting a studio view onto a coloured page by keying its sweep let the page colour leak into frosted packs (their pale film reads close to the sweep): a green page tinted the packs green. Fill the holes in the object mask (anything the product encloses is product) before blending, and check every coloured page at 100% for tint on the product.
- 2026-11-22: Edge-to-edge images (a family line-up) sat 5 to 26 px under the headline: the overlap guard only checks boxes, and a full-bleed image has no inner air. `engine/gap_check.py` now measures the ink gap on the rendered panels (45 px minimum, 60 px target at 1920 wide).
- 2026-11-22: Behance panels were designed as separate slides; stacked with no gap, a run of cream pages each restarting with kicker + headline read as disconnected, and uneven bottoms (20 to 120 px) made cramped seams. Fix: one ground per chapter (a colour change = new chapter), even 72 px air on same-ground seams, bridges only where content already joins; `seam_check.py` measures it. Also: a flat on a coloured page must be keyed (white paper out), never multiplied (tints the art) or set on a card (its own ground).
- 2026-11-22: Two flats of one box (sleeve and drawer) were each fitted to their own slot, so they came out at different scales and read as unrelated. Flats of one structure share one mm scale (`flats_true_scale`: real mm from the SVG width). A line-up canvas must have the same shape as its slot on the page, and each tier should fill the width; otherwise the packs shrink inside dead margins.
- 2026-11-22: Two-view pages (a closed and an open box, a unit and its flat) read as gappy when one view sits in a small box at mid-height and the other in a wide one. Split the page into equal halves with labels on one line; the same object in two states must keep one scale (match the box body, not the frame) and stand on one floor.
- 2026-11-22: A one-scroll case study with several product lines needs a beat between chapters. Full title pages after a film read as dead stops; a short chapter strip (about a third of a page) works: one repeated device in the brand's own visual language, the line's real packs on it, the chapter set big, its ground equal to the next panel's so it flows into the opener. Use it for every chapter or none: a missing strip on the last chapter was spotted at once.
- 2026-11-22: A small-pack row where each pack is tucked 20% under the next reads as narrow, cut-off packs, especially beside one huge whole pack. Show small packs whole with a small gutter; give a scale-contrast hero the whole pack (bleed one edge only) so its name stays readable.
- 2026-11-22: One ground per chapter, checked by measuring each panel's edge colour, not by eye: a chapter that drifted (one cream page in a purple chapter; cream, kraft and a line colour mixed in a boxes chapter) read as loose. When two pages of a chapter now share a ground, re-run the seam check: the air rule applies to seams that used to be cuts.
